## Description

**InkSight** is an open-source e-ink desk companion that turns a small e-paper display into a quiet, always-visible information surface for your desk.

Instead of another glowing notification feed, it gives you useful, beautiful, AI-generated content in a paper-like form — weather, poetry, AI artwork, briefings, countdowns, and more.

### Why InkSight?

**Useful at a glance** — Weather, countdowns, memos, habits, briefings, and daily prompts. Glance up, get what you need, get back to work.

**28 built-in modes** — From practical dashboards (weather, calendar, habit tracker) to atmospheric content (poetry, Zen quotes, AI-generated artwork). All auto-rotate throughout the day.

**AI-powered content** — Multi-LLM support (DeepSeek, Qwen, Moonshot) generates contextual content based on time, weather, and season. The ArtWall mode creates unique black & white artwork every refresh.

**Voice interaction** — Add a $3 mic + speaker module and talk to it. Ask questions, get voice replies, switch modes by voice. Real-time ASR → LLM → streaming TTS.

**JSON mode system** — Every mode is a JSON file. Create custom modes without writing code. Share them on the Mode Plaza for others to install.

**One-stop website** — Flash firmware from browser, configure online, preview renders, try without hardware. No IDE or CLI needed.

**Open source (MIT)** — Firmware, backend, frontend, mode system — everything is open and extensible.

**Low cost DIY** — ~$30 USD for the full hardware setup (ESP32 + 4.2" e-paper display).

### Try Without Hardware

Visit [inksight.site](https://www.inksight.site) to preview all 28 modes without owning any hardware. When you're ready, follow the step-by-step guide to build your own.

## First Comment (Maker's Comment)

Hi Product Hunt! 👋

I built InkSight because I wanted a screen on my desk that doesn't demand attention — just sits there quietly like a piece of paper, showing something useful or beautiful when I look up.

After months of work, it's grown into something I'm really proud of:

- 28 AI-powered display modes, all defined in pure JSON
- A complete website experience (browser flashing, config, preview, mode sharing)
- Voice interaction that's surprisingly snappy
- An active community making 3D cases and custom PCBs

The project is fully open source under MIT. I'd love your feedback — and if you build one, please share it in our Discord!
